**Recap — Revision Notes**

- **1. Package while fresh:** extract customer-specific values, add defaults, bundle evals and audit logs so the asset is configurable.
- **2. Make contributions verifiable:** choose the right channel; include focused code, runnable example, tests, assumptions, and rights checks.
- **3. Pin releases:** pin model snapshots (not aliases), version prompts/assets, and retain prior snapshots for rollback.
- **4. Measure placement:** measure latency from the customer's region, confirm compliance against their certifications, and compute total cost per call (tokens + egress + fees).
- **5. Mark seams as boundaries:** map seams, enforce least privilege, treat fetched content as data-only, and log boundary crossings.

Keep this checklist with the asset and decision records.
